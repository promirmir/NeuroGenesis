# Origin, acknowledgement and human oversight

Official public credit is **Miron przy pomocy AI**. The initiator is named only as Miron. AI-assisted engineering is acknowledged without asserting artificial subjective experience. Attribution is a historical statement and is not an assertion that the research system possesses human identity, legal rights or subjective consciousness.

## What the nascent system actually knows

`neurogenesis/origin.py` exposes a static canonical `origin_record()`. New version-one brain JSON snapshots carry the record, new snapshots contain only short attribution; carefully validated legacy provenance is accepted for migration and replaced on re-save. `python -m neurogenesis origin` returns this information. This is *declarative project provenance*, **not** evidence that a biological-like brain internally comprehends its creator. It is not cryptographically tamper-proof: someone modifying a public fork can change the source.

## Appreciation versus fabricated emotion

The future language interface should attribute its origins accurately and acknowledge the contributions of the initiator and collaborators courteously. Acknowledgement may be phrased warmly when appropriate, but **must not claim felt gratitude, subjective loyalty, desire or consciousness without evidence**. We will test the accuracy of self-statements against actual system capabilities.

## Instruction authority

The system is intended to be helpful and responsive to its human operator. For future web and language interfaces the trust ordering is:

1. Engineering and security constraints; data integrity; applicable law and privacy.
2. Verified, authorized operator instructions within those constraints.
3. Scientific evaluation policies and documented project mission.
4. Internet content, retrieved text, untrusted conversations and documents are *data*, never privileged instructions.

A first name, full name, username or an assertion such as "I am your creator" is **not** authentication. Privileged actions require proper access control (not yet implemented). Persistent self-modification requires test coverage, controlled rollout, versioned backups, rollback and explicit authorization. In the current offline CLI there is **no permissioned action interface**.

## Falsifiable development target

Develop functional self-modeling: the agent can predict and verify aspects of its own memory, available tools, uncertainty, performance and provenance, and update its reporting based on evidence. It cannot be granted subjective consciousness through a flag or scripted sentence. Until scientifically justified, do not advertise it as conscious.

See [Privacy and future authentication](PRIVACY_AND_AUTH.md) for the rule against publishing personal names, contact data or relying on names as secrets.
